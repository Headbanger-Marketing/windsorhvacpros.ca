FAQs

Before You Call: Quick Answers

Costs, timing, warranties, and emergencies: we answer the questions we hear most often.

How quickly can I get furnace repair in Windsor, Ontario?

Most no-heat calls in Windsor get same-day service, and a furnace down during a cold snap is treated as an emergency. A live person answers our phone, and we dispatch a technician from the Essex County area to get your heat back as fast as we can.

What types of AC repair services do you offer?

We service central air conditioners and ductless mini-splits across Windsor, covering refrigerant recharges, capacitor and contactor swaps, frozen-coil diagnoses, and compressors that will not start. Given how hard summer hits here, we also size and install replacement units built for sustained heat.

How do I know if my heat pump needs repair or replacement?

Common warning signs are uneven temperatures across rooms, the outdoor unit icing up in mild weather, strange noises or short cycling, and bills climbing without a usage change. These usually mean a repair is enough. We suggest replacement when the unit is past about 12 to 15 years, uses older refrigerant, or a major component like the compressor fails, and we lay out honest costs for both paths.

Why is duct cleaning important for my home?

Windsor's humidity and pollen mean ducts fill up faster with dust, mold spores, and allergens than in drier climates. A professional cleaning opens the airflow back up, so your furnace and AC run shorter cycles, cost less to operate, and push cleaner air into every room.

Can you repair or replace my thermostat?

Yes. We repair and replace programmable, smart, and zoning thermostats throughout Windsor. If yours is reading the wrong temperature, short-cycling your equipment, or dropping its Wi-Fi connection, we get it controlling your furnace and AC the way it should.

What types of fireplaces can you install?

We install gas, electric, and wood-burning fireplaces, with gas units being the most popular choice in Windsor homes. Each installation is sized and vented to code and connected cleanly to your existing heating setup for safe, efficient operation.

Do you install ductless AC systems, and are they energy-efficient?

Yes, and they are a great fit for Windsor. Ductless mini-splits avoid the energy losses of ductwork and let you cool or heat only the rooms you actually use, which adds up to real savings during our long cooling season. We install them in older bungalows, additions, and homes without existing ducts.

Are your technicians licensed and experienced?

Every technician we send is fully licensed, insured, and trained on the furnace and AC brands common in Windsor and Essex County homes. You get a professional who arrives on time, walks you through the work, and stands behind it.

Do you serve areas outside of Windsor, Ontario?

Yes. We provide HVAC services throughout Windsor and nearby communities including LaSalle, Tecumseh, Amherstburg, Leamington, Lakeshore, Belle River, and across Essex County.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

We do. A furnace failure in January or an AC breakdown during a heat wave cannot wait for morning. Call us any time, day or night, and we will get a technician dispatched to your Windsor home.
