About Us

Windsor's Own HVAC Crew, From the Detroit River to Essex County

We sit closer to Detroit than to Toronto, and our weather behaves like it. Long, humid summers that push air conditioners into overtime, January cold snaps that test any furnace, and a housing mix that runs from century homes in Walkerville to new builds in LaSalle. We're the local team that keeps all of it comfortable.

Our Story

Based on Ouellette Avenue, Working Across Essex County

We're a family-operated HVAC company working out of downtown Windsor, serving homeowners from Amherstburg up to Lakeshore and from Tecumseh out to Leamington. Every job is run by people who actually live and work here, not a dispatch center reading off a script.

Windsor has always been a working city, built on auto manufacturing, cross-border trade, and families who've stayed in the same neighbourhoods for generations. The homes we service reflect that. Century brick houses in Walkerville and Sandwich with original plaster walls and aging ductwork. Postwar bungalows in Riverside and Forest Glade with furnaces that have been holding on since the eighties. Raised ranches and two-storeys across East Windsor, and the newer builds stretching out into LaSalle, Tecumseh, and Belle River. Each one calls for a different approach, and that's exactly what we're set up to deliver.

Being the southernmost city in Canada means our cooling season runs longer and harder than almost anywhere else in Ontario. July and August humidity off Lake Erie and Lake St. Clair keeps air conditioners cycling for weeks on end, and when a cold snap rolls through in January, the wind funnelling down the Detroit River cuts right through under-insulated walls. That's why we cover the full system, furnace installation and repair, central and ductless AC, cold-climate heat pumps, fireplace installation, duct cleaning, and smart thermostat upgrades, instead of picking one specialty and handing off the rest to someone else.

We're not a call center routing jobs to whoever happens to be closest. When you phone (519) 258-3741, you're speaking with people who know the difference between a muggy August afternoon in Leamington and a cold, windy January morning in Amherstburg. We drive past the homes we service on the way to the grocery store, and we'd rather do the job properly the first time than hear about it again at the rink or the farmers' market.

Our Commitment

How We Handle Every Job in Windsor & Essex County

Every furnace, air conditioner, and heat pump we install gets sized to the actual home using a real load calculation, not a rule-of-thumb guess that leaves you sweating through August or running the furnace nonstop in January. Our technicians are licensed and insured, we follow Ontario building and TSSA safety codes on every install, and we verify refrigerant charge, airflow, and thermostat calibration on every service call. Whether it's a ductless mini-split going into a converted Walkerville attic or a high-efficiency furnace replacing a thirty-year-old unit in a Forest Glade bungalow, the standard doesn't move.

We also believe in straight talk. You'll get a clear explanation of what's wrong, honest options laid out before any work starts, and upfront pricing that doesn't change when the invoice arrives. No inflated diagnoses, no pressure to replace equipment that still has good years left in it, and no surprise charges. In a tight, cross-border community like Windsor, where half our customers already know the other half, our reputation is the whole business, and we protect it on every job.
